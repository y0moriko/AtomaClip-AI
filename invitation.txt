
🚀 Big News: Atoma Clip Beta is LIVE!

Ever feel like you’re saving a million bookmarks or highlights but never actually doing anything with them? We’ve built something to fix that. 🧠

What is Atoma Clip?
It’s an AI-powered Chrome Extension and research assistant. It doesn’t just store your links; it actually understands the specific insights you highlight while you browse.

What can you do in this Beta?

Smart Chrome Capture: Highlight text on any website, right-click, and clip it directly to your "Personal Vault" or a specific "Collection."

AI Auto-Sort: Stop worrying about folders. Let the AI analyze and organize your clips into the right projects for you.

Ask Your Research: This is the magic part. Inside the dashboard, you can chat with your saved data. Ask: "What are the recurring patterns here?" and the AI will answer based ONLY on the clips you've saved.

How to join:

Log in at: https://atomaclip-ai-production.up.railway.app
