
**Subject: 🚧 Progress Update: AtomaClip AI (MVP Stage)**

Hey everyone! 👋 I wanted to share a quick progress report on **AtomaClip AI**.

**The Goal:** 
Stop "Bookmark Hoarding" by letting users save specific insights, not just URLs.

**Current Status (Built in just 4 hours!):**
✅ **Chrome Extension:** Successfully built a Manifest V3 extension that captures highlighted text and page metadata via a simple right-click.
✅ **The Dashboard:** A minimalist Next.js gallery is live, featuring "Atomic Cards" with serif typography optimized for research readability.
✅ **The Database:** Fully connected to Supabase and Prisma with a scalable schema ready for high-velocity research.

**Current Hurdles:**
I’ve spent the last hour wrestling with some Google AI SDK naming quirks and model availability (Gemini 1.5 vs. 2.0). Because of this, the AI auto-tagging and semantic search are still in "beta" and might be a bit unstable in this demo. Apologies that it’s not 100% polished yet, but the core **"Atomic Loop" (Clip → Save → View)** is fully functional!

**What's Next:**
Once I've launched this initial version, I will proceed to the next challenge: **Inviting my target users** (content creators and researchers) to the platform. My goal is to discover firsthand whether AtomaClip effectively solves the "Bookmark Hoarding" problem and actually kills the need for 40+ open tabs.
